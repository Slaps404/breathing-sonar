SONAR BREATHING RECORDER  (Mac only, 4 to 20 minutes, you choose)

Your laptop plays a very high-pitched sound (most adults can't hear it) and records the echo
off your chest. We are teaching it to tell breathing from a held breath.
Only the echo recording is saved. It stays on your laptop until you send it.

1. Right-click "Start Recorder.command" > Open > Open.
   (A normal double-click gets blocked by macOS the first time.)
2. Type your first name, then pick how much to help:
     1. Quick       2 takes, about 4 min   (seated, then an empty chair)
     2. Helpful     5 takes, about 13 min  (adds close, far, and moving around)
     3. Extra mile  8 takes, about 20 min  (adds slouched, noisy room, lying down)
   Click Allow when macOS asks about the microphone.
3. Laptop on a table, speaker volume about half, no headphones or AirPods.
4. Click the  ◌  icon in the menu bar (top-right of the screen) > Next take.
   A box tells you how to sit. Click Start.
5. Hands off the laptop. A banner at the top tells you what to do:
     BREATHE NORMALLY = just breathe
     HOLD BREATH      = hold until the banner changes (20 s)
     MOVE AROUND      = fidget like normal (shift, scratch, look around)
     STEP AWAY        = leave the room until it says SAVED
   It saves by itself at the end. Then click  ◌  > Next take again, until it says all done.
6. Choose Quit from the  ◌  menu. A zip appears on your Desktop: send it back.

Done early and want to give more? Click  ◌  > How much to help > pick a bigger one.
The takes you already did count.
If the banner says "redo", Next take repeats that take (close other apps, unmute, volume up).
