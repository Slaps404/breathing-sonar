SONAR BREATHING RECORDER  (Mac only, about 10 minutes)

Your laptop plays a very high-pitched sound (most adults can't hear it) and records the echo
off your chest. We are teaching it to tell breathing from a held breath.
Only the echo recording is saved. It stays on your laptop until you send it.

1. Right-click "Start Recorder.command" > Open > Open.
   (A normal double-click gets blocked by macOS the first time.)
2. Type your first name when asked. Click Allow when macOS asks about the microphone.
3. Set up:
   - Laptop on a table, speaker volume about half, no headphones or AirPods.
   - Sit facing the screen. Write down roughly how far your chest is from the screen.
4. Click the  ◌  icon in the menu bar (top-right of the screen):
   - Distance: pick the one closest to where you sit.
   - Start guided take.
5. Hands off the laptop. A banner at the top tells you what to do:
     BREATHE NORMALLY = just breathe
     HOLD BREATH      = hold until the banner changes (20 s)
   Keep still the whole time. About 3 minutes. It saves by itself at the end.
6. If you have time, do 2 more takes at different distances (close ~30 cm, farther ~70 cm).
   Set the Distance in the menu before each one.
7. Choose Quit from the  ◌  menu. A zip appears on your Desktop: send it back.

If the banner says "audio dropouts: redo", close other apps and do that take again.
