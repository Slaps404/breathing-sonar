Hi! Thanks for helping test BreathBuddy.

BreathBuddy is a student project that listens for breathing using your device's speaker and microphone, like a bat uses sound. It plays a very high sound, which most people can't hear, and listens for its echo off your chest.

What you'll do (about 5 minutes):

Sit still and breathe normally, and hold your breath 3 times, for about 20 seconds each. The screen tells you what to do at every step.

Only hold your breath as long as it feels fine. Skip this test if holding your breath isn't safe for you (for example, heart or lung problems, or you're pregnant).

Get set up

  1. Find a quiet room.Turn off music, TV and fans if you can. Be the only person or pet near the device.
  2. Sit at a table with the laptop in front of you.Sit up, facing the screen, about one forearm away (elbow to fingertips, 30-40 cm). Keep the laptop open on the table, not on your lap.
  3. Stand the phone up on a table, screen facing you.Lean it against something (a book, a cup). Sit facing it, about one forearm away (elbow to fingertips, 30-40 cm), with the phone at chest height if you can. Don't hold it.
  4. Turn the volume up to about 3/4.No headphones, earbuds or Bluetooth speakers: the sound must come from the device itself. Turn off silent mode.
  5. Mac only: set Mic Mode to Standard.Click Control Center (top right, two switches icon) > Mic Mode > Standard. (If you don't see Mic Mode, skip this.)
  6. Keep this page open the whole time.Don't switch apps or lock the screen until it says "Done".

Next, your browser will ask to use the microphone. Please tap Allow.

Sound check

Sit still and quiet for a few seconds while your device checks that it can hear its own sound.
