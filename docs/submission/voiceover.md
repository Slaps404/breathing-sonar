# Script: what to say (video, about 3:00)

Three kinds of recording. Read plainly, at talking speed. Nothing in the video should be silent except the alarm
and the phone call, which must be heard.

- **OPEN and CLOSE:** you on camera, talking to the lens. Film on the phone, landscape, chest up, quiet room.
- **VO 1-7:** voice only, played over the footage. Record on the phone (Voice Memos), one file per line.

Save them into `docs/submission/video/in/` with these names: `open.mov`, `close.mov`, `vo1.m4a` ... `vo7.m4a`.

| File | Over this picture | Say | About |
|---|---|---|---|
| open.mov | You, to camera | "About seventy thousand people in the US died of a drug overdose last year. Three in four of them were alone. An overdose slows your breathing, and then it stops. If nobody is there, nobody knows. So I built BreathBuddy." | 15 s |
| vo1 | Friend's phone: setting it up, sending the link | "A friend sets it up in a minute and texts you a link. There's nothing to install." | 6 s |
| vo2 | Laptop: link opens, Start, breathing with the cloud | "You open it, press Start, and breathe with the cloud. The laptop speaker plays a sound too high to hear, and the microphone listens for the echo off your chest. Those slow breaths show it where you are." | 13 s |
| vo3 | "Breathing with you", then the start of the hold | "Now it's watching. It knows how much my chest moves when I breathe normally. Here I hold my breath, which is our safe stand-in for breathing that has stopped. This part is real time, with no cuts." | 13 s |
| (none) | "Are you okay?" appears, beeps | Leave the alarm sound. | 2 s |
| vo4 | The six second countdown | "It asks if I'm okay. I get six seconds to tap. I don't." | 5 s |
| (none) | Phone rings, the voice message plays | Leave the call sound. | 15 s |
| vo5 | The signal chart or panel, line dropping during a hold | "Here's what it sees. This line is how much my chest is moving at breathing speed. When I hold my breath it drops to a fraction of normal, and that drop raises the alarm." | 11 s |
| vo6 | Limits card | "This morning it caught every breath hold I tried, live, in a quiet room: eleven out of eleven. On our harder recordings, with me moving around, twenty-three of twenty-nine. That's one person and one laptop." | 14 s |
| vo7 | Limits card, second half | "Today it detects breathing that stops. Catching breathing that's only slowing down is the next step." | 7 s |
| close.mov | You, to camera | "It's a prototype, not a medical device, and so far it has only been tested on me. But it runs in a browser, on a laptop people already have. BreathBuddy. So that someone knows." | 12 s |

- "Eleven out of eleven" is today's live app runs up to 9:25 am. If more runs are done before recording, say the new count, and say it honestly if one missed.
