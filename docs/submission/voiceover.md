# Voiceover script (video, about 3:00)

Read slowly and plainly. About 290 words, which is roughly 2 minutes of speech; the rest is the demo playing in silence.
Record on a phone in a quiet room, one file per numbered line, so each can be placed and redone separately.

| # | Over this picture | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening animation (the 75.5% card) | "About seventy thousand people in the United States died of a drug overdose last year. Three in four of them were alone. An overdose slows your breathing, and then stops it. If nobody is there, nobody knows." |
| 2 | Friend's phone: setting it up and sending the link | "BreathBuddy is a web page that listens for your breathing, and phones a friend if it stops. A friend sets it up and sends you a link. There is nothing to install." |
| 3 | Laptop: link opens, Start, breathing with the cloud | "Press Start and breathe with the cloud for about half a minute. The laptop speaker plays a sound too high to hear. The microphone picks up the echo off your chest, and those slow breaths show it where your chest is." |
| 4 | "Breathing with you" | "Now it is watching. It has learned how much my chest moves when I breathe normally." |
| 5 | Just before the hold | "I'm going to hold my breath. That is our safe stand-in for breathing that has stopped." |
| - | The hold, "Are you okay?", the countdown | (silence: let the alarm sound be heard) |
| 6 | "Calling" screen | "It noticed in a few seconds, asked if I was okay, and gave me six seconds to answer. I didn't, so it called." |
| - | Phone rings, the voice message plays | (silence: let the call be heard) |
| 7 | The signal panel, line dropping during a hold | "Here is what it sees. This is how much my chest is moving at breathing speed. When I hold my breath it drops to a fraction of normal, and that drop raises the alarm." |
| 8 | Limits card or the real-recording chart | "This morning it caught eight out of eight breath holds, live, in a quiet room. On our harder recordings, with movement, twenty-three of twenty-nine. That is one person and one laptop. It detects breathing stopping. Catching breathing that is only slowing is the next step. It is a prototype, not a medical device." |
| 9 | Title card | "BreathBuddy. So that someone knows." |

- Line 8: update "eight out of eight" if more live runs are done, and add the 10-run test result if it exists (`node tools/final-test.js`).
- Name: assumes the app is called BreathBuddy (the domain). Change lines 2 and 9 if not.
