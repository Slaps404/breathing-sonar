Tone check

SNR = reflected tone vs background noise at a nearby silent frequency. Under ~20 dB the speaker or mic can't handle that frequency.

Quality = how clearly the motion repeats like breathing (0-1, 3-42 /min). Rate shows once quality is over the slow alarm's clarity bar (DSP.SLOW.q) and 15 s of data exist. Stop downloads the run as JSON for node tools/replay.js.
